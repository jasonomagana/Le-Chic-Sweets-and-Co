Welcome to Le Chic Sweets & Co.

Located in Corona, CA, Le Chic Sweets & Co. specializes in adding a touch of sweetness to every occasion. As a family-owned bakery, we pour love, creativity, and care into every treat we make — from custom cakes to classic pastries — ensuring your special moments are as memorable as they are delicious.

Sweetly Made for You

Cakes for Every Celebration

From birthdays to weddings and everything in between, our custom cakes are designed to match your vision and taste. Whether you’re looking for something elegant and timeless or fun and festive, we’ll craft a beautiful centerpiece that’s as delightful to eat as it is to admire.

Sweet Treats for Every Gathering

Our menu extends far beyond cakes — we bake an irresistible variety of desserts perfect for any celebration. Enjoy soft, chewy cookies, rich brownies, and delicate cupcakes made fresh with high-quality ingredients and a whole lot of heart.

Traditional Mexican Pastries

We’re proud to share a taste of tradition through our authentic Mexican pastries. From flanes to other beloved sweets, each pastry is made with recipes that have been passed down and perfected, bringing comfort and nostalgia to every bite.
